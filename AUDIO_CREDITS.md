# External animal recordings

The recordings listed below are used under CC0 1.0. Their dedication is
separate from the LGPL license of Animania code and upstream resources.
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Recordings were trimmed, downmixed to mono, lightly denoised, high/low-pass
filtered, loudness-adjusted, faded at the boundaries and encoded as 44.1 kHz
Ogg Vorbis. No pitch shifting was applied. Freesound sources use the public
high-quality MP3 previews; OpenGameArt sources use the attached audio files.
See tools/external-audio.json for source checksums, URLs and edit ranges.

## Cooper Creek 20160313_014852 solitary wolf howl very clear.wav
Author: betchkal
Source: https://freesound.org/people/betchkal/sounds/500646/
License: CC0 1.0
Files under assets/animania/sounds/external/: wolves/howl1.ogg, wolves/howl2.ogg

The wolf howls are excerpts of a solitary wild wolf recorded in Alaska.
They are used only for adult wolves, not dogs or foxes. Dog recordings
are not substituted for wolf vocalizations. Existing hurt/death sounds
remain in use where this batch has no suitable new recording.
